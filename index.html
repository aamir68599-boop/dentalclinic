OUR STORY

About RealTooth

RealTooth began more than two decades ago with a simple observation: most people in Lucknow did not avoid the dentist because of cost — they avoided the dentist because of fear. Dr. Amit Anand and Dr. Arpita Anand set out to build a clinic where that fear had no place, and put the promise on the signboard itself: Painless Dentistry by Experts.

That first clinic has grown into five centres across the city — Hazratganj, Gomti Nagar (Vibhuti Khand), Gomti Nagar Extension, Aliganj and Mahanagar — run by a team of more than sixty dentists and specialists covering every branch of dentistry: root canal treatment, implantology, orthodontics, cosmetic dentistry, paediatric care and full-mouth rehabilitation. Over 15,000 patients have been treated across the chain, and the clinics hold a 4.9 rating built from thousands of public reviews on Google, Practo and Justdial.

Two things have stayed constant through that growth. First, technology in the service of comfort: in-house 3D imaging for precise diagnosis, CAD/CAM digital workflows for crowns and implants, and laser dentistry that makes many procedures gentler and faster. Second, transparency: every patient receives a clear treatment plan and a written cost estimate before any work begins, with EMI options available on major treatments so the right treatment is never postponed for the wrong reason.

Whether you are coming in for a routine clean-up, a long-delayed root canal, or a complete smile makeover, the promise at every RealTooth clinic is the same one made twenty years ago: expert care, honestly priced, and genuinely painless.

HONEST ADVICE

How to Choose a Dental Clinic in Lucknow

Use this checklist for any clinic you consider — including ours. A good clinic will pass every point without hesitation.

1

Check reviews across more than one platform

A clinic can look good on a single website. Cross-check its rating on Google, Practo and Justdial together — consistent scores from thousands of reviewers are hard to fake and tell you how patients are treated after the billing is done.

2

Ask who will actually treat you

General dentistry, root canals, implants and braces are different specialities. For anything beyond a check-up, ask whether a specialist — an endodontist, implantologist or orthodontist — will handle your case, and what their experience is.

3

Look at sterilisation before you look at the interiors

A beautiful waiting room means little if instruments are not autoclaved between patients. Good clinics are proud to show you their sterilisation process.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know which treatment I need?+

Our specialists will conduct a thorough examination and explain your options clearly. We recommend booking a consultation so we can assess your specific needs and provide a tailored treatment plan.

Is treatment at RealTooth really painless?+

Yes, we use advanced techniques, modern equipment, and gentle protocols to ensure your comfort. Our doctors are trained to minimize discomfort at every step, from local anesthesia to post-treatment care.

How much do dental treatments cost?+

The cost varies depending on the complexity of the procedure. We believe in complete transparency and will provide you with a written estimate before starting any treatment.

Do you offer EMI or payment plans?+

Yes, we offer easy EMI options on major treatments so that you don't have to postpone essential dental care due to financial constraints.

Do I need an appointment or can I walk in?+

While we do accept walk-ins based on availability, we highly recommend booking an appointment in advance to reduce your wait time and ensure you get a slot at your preferred time.

What happens at my first visit?+

Your first visit includes a comprehensive dental examination, digital X-rays if necessary, and a detailed discussion about your oral health and treatment options.

How often should I get a dental check-up?+

We generally recommend a dental check-up and professional cleaning every six months to maintain optimal oral health and catch any potential issues early.

Which of your five clinics should I choose?+

All our clinics offer the same high standard of care. You can choose the one that is most convenient for you. If you're unsure, use our "We Will Guide You" feature or call our hotline.
